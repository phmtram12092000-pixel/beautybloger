Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • GlowEssence Cosmetics

1. Commitment to Your Privacy

At GlowEssence, we value the trust you place in us when sharing your personal data. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is processed and protected, and the choices you have regarding your personal information under GDPR, CCPA, and international data privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you or automatically as you navigate our platform:

  • Personal Contact Data: Name, shipping address, email address, and phone number when placing orders or subscribing to newsletters.
  • Payment Details: Processed securely via encrypted third-party payment gateways; we never store your full payment card details on our servers.
  • Browsing & Device Information: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and page visit metrics collected through cookies and analytics tags.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Fulfill, deliver, and support your orders and purchases.
  • Improve website functionality, page speed, and catalog personalization.
  • Send order confirmation notices and, with your explicit consent, promotional beauty updates.
  • Detect, prevent, and protect against fraudulent transactions and abuse.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain cart sessions and site security. With your permission via our Cookie Banner, we also utilize anonymized performance cookies to evaluate store traffic. You can adjust your cookie preferences at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in our footer.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request complete deletion of your records. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy compliance team at privacy@glowessence.example.com.
